1. Overview

StepBattle is a walking activity application that allows users to track daily steps, participate in walking challenges and tournaments, and view leaderboards. This Privacy Policy explains what data StepBattle collects, how it is used, and what choices users have.

2. Data We Collect

StepBattle may collect and process the following categories of data:

  • Account data: email address, user ID, display name, authentication provider, and profile information needed to create and manage an account.
  • Step count data: daily step count and historical step count data authorized by the user through supported sources such as Huawei Health / Health Kit, Health Connect, or Google Fit.
  • Challenge and tournament data: participation status, leaderboard position, total steps for active challenges, and synchronization timestamps.
  • Technical data: device/app information required for authentication, synchronization, diagnostics, crash prevention, and app security.

3. Huawei Health / Health Kit Data

StepBattle requests access only to step count data needed for walking progress, recent step history, tournaments, and leaderboard calculations. The app reads step data only after the user explicitly authorizes access through Huawei Health / Health Kit.

StepBattle does not request access to heart rate, blood pressure, SpO2, sleep, body fat, reproductive health, medical indicators, location, or other sensitive health categories unless a future version clearly explains and requests such access separately.

StepBattle does not write step data or other health data to Huawei Health.

4. How We Use Data

StepBattle uses collected data to:

  • authenticate users and maintain user accounts;
  • show daily step progress and recent step history;
  • synchronize steps for active tournaments and walking challenges;
  • calculate rankings and leaderboards;
  • maintain app security, prevent misuse, and troubleshoot technical issues;
  • improve the reliability and usability of the app.

5. Data Sharing

StepBattle does not sell users' personal data or health data. Data may be processed through service providers used by the app, such as authentication, database, cloud synchronization, analytics, and platform services, only for the purposes described in this Policy.

Leaderboard-related information, such as display name and step totals for a tournament, may be visible to other users participating in the same challenge.

6. Legal Basis and User Consent

StepBattle processes account and app data to provide the service requested by the user. Step count data from Huawei Health / Health Kit, Health Connect, or Google Fit is processed only after the user grants permission through the relevant platform authorization screen.

7. Data Retention

StepBattle keeps account, tournament, and step synchronization records only as long as needed to provide app functionality, maintain leaderboards, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, and improve app reliability. Users may request account or data deletion by contacting the developer.

8. User Choices and Permissions

Users can:

  • deny or revoke health/step permissions in device or platform settings;
  • choose a different step data source inside the app, where supported;
  • request account or data deletion by contacting the developer;
  • stop using the app and uninstall it at any time.

9. Security

StepBattle uses reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect user data. However, no electronic transmission or storage system is completely secure, and absolute security cannot be guaranteed.

10. Children's Privacy

StepBattle is not intended for children under the age required by applicable law to use online services without parental consent. If the developer becomes aware that a child has provided personal data without appropriate consent, reasonable steps will be taken to delete that data.

11. International Processing

Depending on the services used by StepBattle, data may be processed on servers located outside the user's country or region. The developer takes reasonable steps to ensure that such processing is performed in accordance with applicable data protection requirements.

12. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. The updated version will be published on this page with a new “Last updated” date.
